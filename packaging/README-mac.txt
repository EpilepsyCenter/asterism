Installing ASTERISM on a Mac

1. Drag ASTERISM onto the Applications folder.

2. The first time only: ASTERISM is free and not signed with an Apple developer
   certificate, so macOS asks before opening it.
   - Open ASTERISM from Applications. macOS says it can't check it; click Done.
   - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to "ASTERISM was blocked",
     click Open Anyway, and confirm with your password.
   After that it opens normally.
